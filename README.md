## About me

I'm Sahil Khan. My work spans prosthetic devices, spacecraft mechanisms, CAD automation, embedded systems, and computational modeling. At the U.S. Naval Research Laboratory, I worked on mechanisms and NX Open automation; at Estée Lauder, I worked on manufacturing validation. I document the requirements, design decisions, evidence, and limits behind each project.
